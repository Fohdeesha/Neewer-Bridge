PRODUCT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Thank you for purchasing the TL120C multifunctional stick light!

The TL120C stick light is an innovative product that offers a good selection
of colors and over 25 special effects, including 18 scene effects and 7 pixel
effects. The device can be easily controlled via APP, directly through the
control panel, 2.4G, and DMX professional lighting control, catering to the
needs of both professional and amateur users. Whether you're an experienced
user or a novice, you can easily adjust the lighting to your preference using
any of the multiple control methods available. With its versatility and
user-friendly design, the TL120C stick light is a must-have tool for any
lighting enthusiast.

================================================================================
INSTALLATION
================================================================================

1. Installing on a Stand
   Twist the 1/4" threaded screw located on the top of the stand into the
   1/4" screw hole found at the bottom of the light.

2. Installing with the Power Supply Clamp
   (1) To insert the light body into the supporting bracket, first open the
       latch by pulling it outward in the direction of the arrow.
   (2) Reposition the latch of the support bracket inward (in the direction
       of the arrow) to secure the light body.
   (3) Take the 1/4" screw bolt located at the top of the stand and insert
       it into the screw hole situated at the bottom of the power supply
       clamp. Secure the bolt by twisting it into place.

3. Using the Steel Wire
   Adapt the use of the steel wire to your specific needs when using the
   light.


================================================================================
HOW TO USE
================================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Charging
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To begin charging, connect the power cord to the DC socket. While charging,
the screen will display a charging icon "||||" to indicate the progress of
the charging process. Once the battery is fully charged, the screen will
display a full battery icon and remain on.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Powering On/Off
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To turn the light on using the DC power supply or the built-in lithium
battery, press and hold the "Power" button for 1 second. The display will
show "UNLOCK". Then, rotate any knob three times to activate the light.
While the light is powered on, press and hold the "Power" button for 1
second to switch off the light.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standby
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When the light is powered on, short press the ON/OFF button "Power" to enter
standby mode. Press again to exit this mode.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Brightness Adjustment
------------------------------------------------------------------------
In any mode, rotate the brightness adjustment knob to adjust the brightness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Color Temperature Adjustment
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen displays "CCT" to switch to
color temperature mode. Short press the "Settings Switch" button until the
screen selects the "00K" option, then rotate the settings value adjustment
knob to set the color temperature value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Red/Green Compensation Adjustment
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen displays "CCT" to switch to
color temperature mode. Short press the "Settings Switch" button until the
screen selects the "GM" option, then rotate the setting value adjustment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Saturation Adjustment
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen displays "HSI" and switches to
HSI mode. Then, short press the "Settings Switch" button until the "S"
option is selected on the screen. Finally, rotate the settings value
adjustment dial to set the saturation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hue Adjustment (with touch panel)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "Curve Adj/Lock" button in any mode, and the lock indicator
light will not light up. Then, adjust the hue by sliding your finger on the
touch panel. Short press the "Curve Adj/Lock" button again, and the lock
indicator light will light up. The touch panel is now locked and disabled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hue Adjustment (with button)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen displays "HSI" and switches to
RGB mode. Then, short press the "Settings Switch" button until the "H"
option is selected on the screen. Finally, rotate the settings value
adjustment dial to set the hue.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scene Adjustment
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen displays "FX" and switches to
scene mode. Then, rotate the settings value adjustment dial until the screen
switches to a different scene.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scene Modes (18 types)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lightning
- Paparazzi
- Defective Bulb
- Explosion
- Welding
- CCT Flash
- Hue flash
- CCT Pulse
- HUE Pulse
- Cop car
- Candlelight
- HUE loop
- CCT loop
- INT loop
- TV screen
- Fireworks
- Party
- Music

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel Effect Adjustment
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen displays "PIXEL" and switches to
pixel effect mode. Then, short press the "Settings Switch" button or rotate
the settings value adjustment dial until the desired effect is displayed on
the screen.

NOTE: In pixel effect mode, you can scan the QR code to download the app and
adjust the specific setting for the selected effect.

Pixel Effects (7 types):
- Color Replacement
- Color Wheel Rotation
- Single Color Movement
- Dual Color Movement
- Three-Color Movement
- Rainbow Color
- Point Flame Control

------------------------------------------------------------------------
RGBCW Mode
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen displays "RGBCW" and switches to
RGBCW mode. Then, short press the "Settings Switch" button until the setting
is selected on the screen. Finally, rotate the value adjustment dial to set.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gel Adjustment
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen displays "GEL" and switches to
gel mode. Then, short press the "Settings Switch" button until the setting
is selected on the screen. Finally, rotate the dial to set the value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Curve Adjustment
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Long press the "Curve Adj/Lock" button until the screen displays the curve
adjustment interface, then use the settings dial to adjust the curve.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
XY Coordinate Mode
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "M" button until the screen switches to XY coordinate mode.
Short press the "Settings Switch" button to select the XY decimal point
option, then use the settings dial to set the value.

NOTE: XY Value Range (0.0001~0.8000).


================================================================================
DMX CONNECTION
================================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------
DMX Connection (Connected to a console)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When the light stick is connected to a console, the "DMX" indicator lights
up, indicating that it is in DMX mode. Rotate the settings value adjustment
dial to adjust the DMX channel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Channel Selection
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When the channel number of DMX console and that of DMX control box are the
same, you can use the console to adjust various functions. This is suitable
for one light or multiple light connection.

* In DMX mode, the channel number is 1 by default.
* In DMX mode, the channel range can be set to 1-512.
* After the DMX signal is lost, the last signal will be remembered and can
  be used to control the lights.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
DMX Console Control
------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE: In DMX mode, please refer to the "TL60 RGB DMX Channel Table" in the
appendix for relevant control schemes.

1. Connect the DMX cable to the light.
2. Connect the XLR to RJ45 adapter cable to the DMX cable.
3. Connect the DMX standard console to the light.

NOTE: The XLR to RJ45 adapter cable and DMX console are not included in
the package.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Connecting a Single Light
------------------------------------------------------------------------
DMX512 console OUTPUT -> DMX cable -> TL120C light

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Connecting Multiple Lights
------------------------------------------------------------------------
DMX512 console OUTPUT -> DMX cable -> TL120C light 1 (DMX Out) -> TL120C light 2

------------------------------------------------------------------------
XLR to RJ45 Adapter Cable Pin Definition
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To ensure compatibility with the lights, please check the DMX interface
definition of the XLR to RJ45 adapter cable before purchase, as shown
in the diagram.

  PIN1: Data (+)
  PIN2: Data (-)
  PIN7: Ground
  PIN8: Ground

------------------------------------------------------------------------
DMX Channel Adjustment (Not connected to a console)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When connected to Infinity or 2.4G, short press the "DMX" button to enter
the DMX channel adjustment interface. Then, rotate the settings value
adjustment dial to configure the DMX channel.

Short press the "DMX" or "M" button to exit the DMX adjustment interface.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Connecting via 2.4G
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "**/2.4G" button until the indicator light "2.4G" lights up
to enter the 2.4G connection mode. Press and hold the "**/2.4G" button for
1 second until the screen displays "CH", then rotate the settings value
adjustment dial to set the channel value.

NOTE: In 2.4G mode, you can set multiple LED lights to the same channel
(CH8 for example). When you adjust one LED light, the other lights sharing
the same channel will also change in sync.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Connecting via the Infinity Connection Mode
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short press the "**/2.4G" button until the indicator light "**" lights up
to enter Infinity connection mode. If you want to control the device via
your phone, you need to connect the device from the "NEEWER" APP. For
further guidance, please scan the QR code on the APP prompt card and refer
to the "APP Guide" page.

NOTE: Before connecting via the APP, please reset the Infinity connection.
To do so, simply press and hold the middle button on the dial "**/2.4G"
for 5 seconds.


================================================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES
================================================================================

1. DO NOT place the product in an environment with a temperature higher
   than 40°C.
2. DO NOT drop the product on the ground, subject it to collisions, or
   strong impact.
3. DO NOT stare directly at the light when using it, as direct light can
   cause visual impairment.
4. DO NOT use the product in a dusty or humid environment.
5. DO NOT modify the circuit board or disassemble the product without
   authorization.
6. Please unplug the power cord when not in use.
7. If not used for a long time, store the product in a well-ventilated, dry
   area, away from heat and magnetic fields.
